Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an emergency?

You can report emergencies through the CERAS platform or by calling the appropriate emergency number: 191 for police, 192 for fire, 193 for ambulance.

Is CERAS available 24/7?

Yes, CERAS emergency services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our response coordination is always active.

How can I get emergency alerts?

You can subscribe to emergency alerts through the CERAS app or website. Alerts are sent via SMS, push notifications, and web notifications.

Can I volunteer with CERAS?

Yes! We welcome trained volunteers. Contact our partnerships team at partnerships@ceras.gov.gh to learn about volunteer opportunities.

What information do I need to provide when calling?

Provide your location, the nature of the emergency, number of people affected, and any immediate safety concerns. Follow dispatcher instructions carefully.

How do I provide feedback?

Use the contact form above to send feedback directly to CERAS. We value your input and use it to improve our services.
