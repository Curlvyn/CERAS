Community Responders

First Response Team

Locally trained volunteers who provide immediate assistance during emergencies. Our community responders are equipped with first aid knowledge and are available to help neighbors during critical situations. They act as the bridge between citizens and official emergency services.

2,500+ Volunteers
24/7 Availability
50+ Districts

Coordination Hubs

Command Centers

Regional command centers staffed by trained volunteers working with emergency services. These hubs coordinate response efforts, manage resource allocation, and ensure real-time communication between responders and emergency management agencies across all regions.

16 Centers
500+ Trained Staff
99.2% Uptime
