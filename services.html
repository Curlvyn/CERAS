CERAS Services

Comprehensive emergency response and alert coordination capabilities

NADMO

National disaster coordination and hazard monitoring for flood, shelter, and response operations across districts.

Ghana Police

Crime reporting, incident tracking, and police response coordination for security incidents and public order alerts.

Ambulance Service

Emergency medical dispatch, patient response tracking, and rapid ambulance coordination across regions.

Fire Service

Fire outbreaks, rescues, hazardous incidents, and firefighter deployment updates from the frontline response network.

Location-Based Services

GPS-integrated features that pinpoint emergency locations on interactive maps. Helps responders navigate quickly, coordinate arrival sequences, and provide precise information to dispatchers and command centers.

Analytics & Reporting

Comprehensive data analysis tools for emergency management. Track response times, incident patterns, resource utilization, and system performance to continuously improve emergency response effectiveness across Ghana.

Key Features

  • 24/7 Availability: Emergency support always active, never interrupted
  • Mobile First Design: Works seamlessly on smartphones and tablets in any condition
  • Multi-Channel Communication: SMS, app notifications, and web alerts ensure message delivery
  • Agency Integration: Connects police, fire, ambulance, and disaster management agencies
  • Volunteer Coordination: Mobilizes trained community volunteers for rapid response support
  • Data Security: Encrypted communication and secure data storage for emergency information
