Tips for a good report

  • Use clear incident titles and location details.
  • Describe hazards, injuries, and affected areas.
  • Upload JPG photos with scene evidence.
  • Give a contact phone or email for follow-up.
  • Report quickly, every second helps responders.

Need faster help?

If the situation is life threatening, call the emergency service numbers immediately before or after submitting the report.
